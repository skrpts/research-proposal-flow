## Capability

Writes a complete research proposal in full prose — title, abstract, introduction, research questions and hypotheses, literature review, methodology, timeline, expected outcomes, ethics, and references — grounded in the gap analysis, generated hypotheses, and methodology assessment. Produces the proposal itself, not a review of the inputs.
